# Story

The background for dialogue and quests. Write dialogue from this file. The
setting comes from the user's own tabletop campaign from childhood; more of
it will come from old notes. Only the facts below are agreed.

## Dragonvow (the world)

- Long ago, an ancient dragon and a holy maiden were in love.
- Their descendants do not know this. People still see dragons as villains.
- The dragon and the maiden have no names yet.

## Tandi (the village)

- The name is a tribute to Fallout 1.
- Tandi is a village near the other villages of the region. The other
  villages work as usual.
- More monsters come near Tandi than before, and the villagers are worried.
- Food is short because there are more monsters.

## The den

- The den holds magic beasts, not natural animals.
- Later: goblins, which are intelligent.

## People

- **Farmer Reed** (male, dutiful). He gives the food quest.
- **The innkeeper** (female, grumpy). She talks but gives no quest. The
  village has no inn building yet; she stands in the village.

## Tone

Humorous but realistic. A classic Japanese RPG feel: mostly positive.

## Language

Dialogue text is written in English. It is the key for a translation later
(keyless i18n, see the dialogue script spec).
